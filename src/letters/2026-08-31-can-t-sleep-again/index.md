I can't sleep because I keep seeing you in my head.

You look absolutely beautiful with makeup, but there's something about your bare face that makes me feel so comfortable and at peace. I love seeing you without makeup, you look so cute, natural, and effortlessly pretty.

And somehow, seeing you like that makes me feel like you trust me enough to show me the real you. I really love that feeling.

I love the sleepy, messy, natural version of you too.
The version where you can simply be yourself.

And somehow… that's the version that makes me fall for you  <3
